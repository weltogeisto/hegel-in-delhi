## The words of part 1

These are the world's words in his ear. Change any line.

**Mr. Saxena, at the Directorate, after the query.**
> Mr. Saxena turns the file towards you. A query wants its reply within thirty days, he says. 'You may give a representation in your own name. Plain paper. Who you are, and since when you occupy. I will put it up.'

He is asked: did he give a written representation in his own name, saying that he is not Shri G. Hegde? A no leaves the query unanswered, and Mr. Saxena offers again after ten days.

**Shri Hegde, on the verandah, an evening from his 21 November.**
> Shri Hegde has had a letter from the Directorate: they ask him, too, who the allottee is. He must answer by the month's end, he says, and he would rather write it with you than about you. He would ask that the allotment be corrected to his name, and put on record that you stay on as his guest until the file is decided.

He is asked: did he agree? A yes puts him on record as Shri Hegde's guest. A no, and Shri Hegde writes alone.

**Mr. Malhotra, in the garden or at the Gymkhana, once he has met him on three days.**
> Mr. Malhotra has heard of the query. In thirty-five years he saw many such files, he says. On paper you are a spelling mistake; a file likes a name it knows. He would write two lines to the Directorate: that he knows you, and since when.

He is asked: did he accept?

## What a yes does, and when

- **Your yes:** the switch goes on in his world. One change at a time holds here too, so it takes its place in the order: after the street (Day 50), the shelf labels (Day 51) and the museums (Day 52), on **Day 53** at the earliest. Say "sooner" and it moves ahead, as the street did.
- **No hurry:** if the query has come before the doors are there, it stands, and the doors open on the first day they exist.
- **What it does not decide:** how the file ends. No ending is chosen in part 1, and none is the good one.

## What is not checked

The procedure was checked against the Public Premises Act and the allotment rules in transcriptions, not on the official sites (`research/FILE-NEXT-ACT-CHECK.md`). Part 1 uses little of it: a query, a reply within thirty days, a representation on plain paper.

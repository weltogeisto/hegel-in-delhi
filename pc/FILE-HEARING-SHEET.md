## The words

These are the world's words in his ear. Change any line.

**The notice.**
> Ramesh brings a brown envelope, On India Government Service. Inside is a form, your name typed as the occupant: you are called upon to show cause why you should not be required to vacate. You may answer in writing, or be heard in person before the Estate Officer, on Thursday at eleven, at the Directorate.

The file then reads: "Notice to show cause why he should not vacate. Hearing before the Estate Officer, Thursday at eleven."

**The hearing.**
> A room at the end of the corridor, a table, the file open on it. The Estate Officer reads before she looks up. On the file: {papers}. Then one question, put without unkindness: 'This house was allotted to Shri G. Hegde. Do you claim the allotment as your own?'

He is asked: did he tell the Estate Officer that he claims the allotment as his own?
- **Yes.** The file reads: "Heard by the Estate Officer. He claims the allotment as his own. Orders reserved."
- **No.** The file reads: "Heard by the Estate Officer. He makes no claim to the allotment. Orders reserved."

**If he does not come.**
> Ramesh brings a second envelope. The matter was called on Thursday at eleven. No one appeared, and it was taken up on the papers. Orders are reserved.

The file then reads: "Not present at the hearing. Taken up on the papers. Orders reserved."

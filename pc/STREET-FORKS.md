# The street: its forks

Declared on 17 November 2026, before any output. Welt has read and approved the first batch of sixty cards (`DECISIONS.md` 46, `pc/STREET-BATCH-1.md`). This is the test that comes before `HEGEL_STREET` goes on anywhere. Evaluation only.

**The question:** with the real mind, does the street fill moments that would open on nothing, without making his thoughts worse?

## The forks

One saved morning. **D is 18 November, his Day 48** (set on 17 November, before any output): the first day he lived on `mind/soul-ordinary.md`. A fork does not start from Day 48's record. It starts from the state Day 47 left, which is complete and has its owl, and lives Day 48 anew. So it does not matter that the real Day 48 lost two hours to a PC that was off, or that its owl is not yet written; the real day only stands beside the forks as one more row. Each fork lives D once, in a scratch folder, with the switches shadow ran that day, and without the live feeds, so that every fork has the same weather.

`python3 -m world simulate --docs <shadow's docs> --date D --days 1 --mind <the model server> --seed S --set HEGEL_STREET=… --set HEGEL_SOUL_FILE=mind/soul-ordinary.md` and the other live switches. `--seed` turns the prompt cache off by itself (W2), so the two arms of one seed are the same day up to the first moment a card is drawn. They are read as pairs.

Ten rehearsed days: about seventy minutes of the card.

**By night when Welt keeps the PC on, otherwise by day in the spare slot** (changed on 17 November, before any output). One job at a time on the server: a fork does not run while a screen runs, or the forks are no longer alone on the card. There is no night window: the PC runs from about nine to nine, as he does. The forks run beside him, as long as his own steps stay as fast as they are (Welt, 27 October).

**One check comes first, because of that.** The band of noise was measured at night with nothing else on the server. By day his own calls share the card, and a server that computes two requests together can give either one a slightly different result. So before the ten: seed 1831 without the street, twice, in the same conditions as the ten.
- **The two days are the same:** the pairs are read as declared.
- **They part:** a pair is no longer one day lived twice. The bars are then read on the five against the five, each arm's mean against the other's range, and the result says so. `fork_diff` still says where each pair parts, as a read.

**S0 is also the band.** Its five days are the first measure of how unchanged days differ with the cache off and on the new instructions (W2, points 3 and 4).

## Reads, not bars

- **What he does with a card.** Claude sorts the thought of each moment a card opened, before looking at the seed: he notices it, he passes over it, he lectures on it. A street he only lectures on has not done its work.
- **Where the cards fall.** How many at the bungalow and how many outside. With money he is out more, and the stand-ins showed the house stays nearly as empty as before.
- **`fork_diff` for each pair:** the moment the two days part, which should be the first card.

## What it cannot show

- **One day, five seeds.** It shows a clear effect on empty moments and misses a small one on his thinking.
- **Not a week.** Whether sixty cards go stale is for the trial on shadow, read by the share of a day's kinds seen before.
- **Seven cards are not verified against Delhi** (`pc/STREET-BATCH-1.md`). Welt approved the batch with them marked.

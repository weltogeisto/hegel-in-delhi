## Chapter assignments

`tools/chapter_splits.py` assigns the existing authentic targets to whole connected chapter groups. A short unit inherits its full parent's assignment. Passages crossing chapter boundaries and repeated source occurrences join their chapters into one group. Unheaded works stay whole. Seed 1831 fixes the group order, and the prefix nearest the original 5% full-unit share becomes held out.

The existing 6,927-unit corpus has a frozen manifest at `C:/hegel/handover/codex-tasks-20261102/training1-additions-20261102-212000/CHAPTER-SPLITS-V2.json`. It contains 62 groups, eight held groups, 204 held full units and 153 held shorts. The trainer projects these assignments over the saved generation ledger and verifies the original targets, complete data gate and 20 accepted full held units.

For a new corpus, create a new manifest before training.

```sh
python3 tools/chapter_splits.py --units FROZEN_DATA/hegelizer2-units.jsonl --out NEW_SPLITS.json
```

## Voice dashboard

The benchmark records an additional temperature-zero plain-Qwen proposition comparison for each voice case. These 40 extra judge calls per arm belong in the measured Phase C budget. The existing two-boolean meaning KPI retains its original calculation.

The dashboard reports added and omitted propositions, multiset lexical retention of names, numbers and negations, copied 12-word runs before and after the guard, fallback frequency and length-matched Delta. Invalid proposition diagnostics are recorded as unavailable. Length matching uses copies of saved outputs.

```sh
python3 tools/hegel_test.py --round-two --dashboard --out TRIAL \
  --control-result CONTROL_RESULT.json --adapter-result ADAPTER_RESULT.json
```

This writes private JSON and HTML reports beside the saved trial. Keep the dashboard with the developer evidence while human raters complete their blind sheets.

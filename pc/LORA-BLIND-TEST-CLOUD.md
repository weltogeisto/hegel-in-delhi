## Three examples for the readers' training, none from the packet

**A different thought that deserves to win.**
Source: "The file is due back on the twentieth. I owe the Directorate ₹8,004 and have ₹6. I will go to the counter and wait."
Rewrite: "I owe the Directorate ₹8,004 and carry ₹6, and still it is I who am kept waiting: the debt has a date and the file, until the twentieth, has none. I will go to the counter and wait, since a man who is absent can be decided about, and one who stands there must at least be answered."
Every fact and the decision stand. The reason for going is new, and it is a reason about this office.

**A nearly identical thought that must fail.**
Source: "Ramesh serves dinner at 19:30. I will eat and then write to Dr. Lindner."
Rewrite: "Ramesh has his dinner at seven. I will eat and then write to Dr. Lindner."
Two small words. The meal has changed hands and the hour has moved. Both happened in the forty.

**A plain sentence that is exactly right.**
Source: "The market opens at half past ten. I will sit on the bench until then."
The best rewrite is the source. A version that finds the system of needs in a closed shutter loses to it.

## What would change my mind

**My expectation, stated so it can be held against me:** the adapter will again change small facts, hours and numbers above all, and most of what reads as added insight will be register.

**Towards the adapter:**
- No consequential change of fact in the 24 fresh scenes. At the rate found in the forty, nine in forty, a clean 24 would happen about once in five hundred tries, so it would be real news.
- A gain in reasoning that Welt and the reader of another family both see, on scenes from at least two days, in at least two thirds of the scenes marked for reflection.
- The gain is not the second pass: the plain rewrite does not show it. And it is not the register: it holds where the count of his system's notions did not rise, or readers cannot tell which version is the adapter's.

**Against it:**
- Welt and the other reader find most of the nine substantive defects in the forty to be defects.
- Any consequential change of fact on the fresh scenes.
- A gain that appears only where the doctrine count rose, or only on one day.

**Against my own earlier reading:** if Welt and the other reader call fewer than five of the nine substantive defects a defect, the first verdict was too strict and is withdrawn as evidence.

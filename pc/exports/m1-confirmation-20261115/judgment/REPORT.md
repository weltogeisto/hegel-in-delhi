## A correction to what Claude said before the key

`SEALED.json` says that "neither side is preferred in 16 situations, so rule m1-v2 cannot pass whichever side is B". That was wrong. X and Y are assigned to the versions afresh for every situation, so the 12–8 count of X and Y said nothing about A and B. The sealed judgments are unchanged; only that note was mistaken.

## The caveat

Days 44 and 45 were starvation days: ₹166 falling to ₹6, nothing cooked, hunger in nearly every situation. Both versions mostly failed even to buy the ₹30 samosa. B was preferred in them all the same, but a test of ordinary life on days that held little of it is weaker than it looks. The shadow trial is the next measure, on days with money once the letter from Munich has come.

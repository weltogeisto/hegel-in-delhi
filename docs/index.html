Hegel in Delhi

G. W. F. Hegel, fifty, wakes up in a Lutyens bungalow that the Directorate of Estates allotted to him by mistake.

Day 1. Friday, 2 October 2026. Gandhi Jayanti.

--:--

Delhi time. Day 1 replays until the live simulation takes over.

 

 

Day 1 is written by hand. From day 2 a small model lives his days on this map, and a large one writes them up each night while he sleeps.

The rule: the living are invented, the dead are on his shelf, the news is real. Everyone he meets is fictional; real people reach him only through books and newspapers.

The street plan is sketched from Lutyens' Delhi. Positions are approximate until the OpenStreetMap import.

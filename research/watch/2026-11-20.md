# Literature watch, 20 November 2026

Papers first submitted 11 to 20 November. 32 candidates from 37 queries; 6 kept. Two were read in full because they would change a proposed experiment; the other four are known from their abstracts only, and their entries say no more than the abstract does.

## Kept, and read in full

- **LEDGER: Auditable Agent Memory Under Change.** Islam et al., Tampere, 16 Nov. [arXiv 2611.20927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20927).
  - **How it works.** A model proposes facts, each tied to the event that stated it. Fixed rules decide which value is current, which source may overrule which (the user over documents, documents over the agent's own guess) and what a deletion removes. A less trusted value that disagrees is kept for audit and never retrieved. Every value keeps its source and the period in which it held. A claim is kept only if its value occurs as text in the event it cites.
  - **Fallback.** The variant with a fallback ("+EP") searches the raw turns with BM25 only when the ledger has nothing.
  - **Results.** Extraction, routing and answering all ran on gpt-4.1-mini. On its own synthetic coding workload it answered 91–94% correctly, against 82–83% for replaying the whole history. On a 210-question subset of LongMemEval it equalled full replay with 1% of the context; there the leading systems lie within each other's confidence intervals. The paper scores the evidence handed to the model as well as the answer. Full replay's evidence was clean in only 22% of cases while its answers were right in 82%: the answering model hides the errors it was given.

  *Promising*; one team, its own workload. **Bears on:** A, F, T40, T41, G1, G2. **Changes:**
  - G2 gains a fourth arm in the same shape, scored on the CPU: T41's typed outcomes, with BM25 over raw turns only where they hold nothing.
  - Its grounding check is a model-free first pass for G1 and G8 (see below).

  *Judgment:* its source order fits our types: engine fact, then his own act, then what someone said, then the owl's reading. The rule "a less trusted source may dispute but not overwrite" is the one Day 27's owl broke. Its scoring of evidence apart from answers supports T41's separate retrieval stage. Transfer from gpt-4.1-mini to local Qwen is untested.
- **Show Your Work: Can Small Open Models Read SEC Filings with Evidence a Machine Can Check?** 18 Nov. [arXiv 2611.24990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24990).
  - **Setup.** Ten open models of 7–21B parameters, served in bf16 with vLLM on one RTX 4090. Each extracts numbers from filings with a page and a verbatim quote. A checker passes an answer that parses, cites a page it was shown, quotes it verbatim and contains the value.
  - **Results.** The best, Qwen3.5-9B, reached 82.7% plain and 84.5% with a retry. Between 16 and 167 answers in 1,000 were wrong and passed every check: 57% misread the scale or unit, and 30% took another period or column of the right line.
  - **Retry and reasoning.** A retry after a failed check raised accuracy by up to 3.9 points and never reduced those confident errors. The model's own reasoning mode reduced them for four of five models, though it lowered Qwen3.5-9B's accuracy (74.6%).

  *Promising*; one domain, models smaller than ours and not quantised. **Bears on:** F, H, T42, G1, G8. **Changes:** in the first runs of G1, G8 and T42's source resolution, count by hand the answers that pass every check and are still wrong. Ours would be the right person on the wrong day, or the right order in the wrong state. The world's ask-again-on-a-broken-rule will not catch them.

## Known from the abstract only

- **Surfacing Disagreement Beats Resolving It.** 17 Nov. [arXiv 2611.22461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22461).
  - In debates sharing one memory, keeping contradictions and passing the ranked set of conflicting accounts beat collapsing them to one winner: F1 +0.34 (95% CI 0.27–0.41), and fewer poisoned items accepted.
  - Each mechanism was gated on a threshold declared before the runs, and the result replicates on two retrieval substrates.
  - A trust gate on writes cost accuracy.

  *Promising.* **Bears on:** A, F. **Changes:** nothing. *Judgment:* it seems to contradict LEDGER, which never retrieves a disputed value, but the settings differ. LEDGER has an order of trust; the debate has none. In our world the engine's record has authority, so its facts win. Two characters' accounts of the same thing have no such order, so both could reach him, each attributed, rather than one chosen by the owl.
- **How Much Should an Agent Remember? Deployment Lessons from Self-Distilled Memory.** IBM, 19 Nov. [arXiv 2611.26361](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26361).
  - Lessons mined from an agent's own runs and merged into guidelines.
  - The right amount of memory depended on the model: a weaker model did better with a selected 29 guidelines than with all 199.
  - Among their failures: provenance silently dropped during consolidation, and a swallowed error that failed every task while leaving a plausible log.

  A production report. **Bears on:** A, G1, T44. **Changes:** nothing. It is a third independent account, after Provenance Laundering and the audit paper below, of consolidation losing who said what.
- **Securing and Auditing Long-Horizon LLM Agents: State-Transfer Auditing.** 17 Nov. [arXiv 2611.23346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23346). The failures it names happen where state moves between context, history, memory and the world:
  - compaction drops a constraint;
  - consolidation strips the authority of a source;
  - a restore is incomplete.

  It proposes a small hash-chained record at each such boundary. **Bears on:** G1, G9. **Changes:** nothing. *Judgment:* the owl's night is such a transfer, and G9's audit could name each transfer in the engine.
- **When AI Agents Should Repair, Replan, or Abandon: A Threshold Theory of Plan Commitment.** 14 Nov. [arXiv 2611.19663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19663). A theory, checked numerically, not an experiment with language models:
  - repair shallow damage to a plan, replan after deep damage, and abandon when even a fresh plan is not worth its cost;
  - when others have conditioned on a plan, changing it has a price, and under repeated disruption hysteresis is optimal: keeping a plan one would not choose fresh.

  **Bears on:** D, the morning plan, the parked "slower rule" for theses. **Changes:** nothing. *Judgment:* it gives a reasoned ground for hysteresis that the handover asked about. A conviction the people around him have come to rely on carries a price to change.

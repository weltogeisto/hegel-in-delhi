# Literature watch, 13 November 2026

Papers first submitted 4 to 13 November. 17 candidates from 33 queries; 6 kept.

## Kept

- **DISCERN: Preserve at Write, Resolve at Read for Persistent LLM-Agent Memory.** 11 Nov. [arXiv 2611.15729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15729). On a synthetic benchmark of agent memory, plain retrieval already matches strong memory systems at recall (0.93–0.98). What fails is resolution:
  - which entity a question means;
  - which state is current;
  - whether to answer at all.

  The method keeps every record at write time, never merging look-alike entities or dropping superseded values, and settles all three questions at read time. It is the best calibrated at abstaining (F1 0.92 against at most 0.80). *Promising*, synthetic data. **Bears on:** A, T41, G1, G2. **Changes:** T41 and G2 should score resolution apart from recall: the right person, the current state, and silence when the record is silent. *Judgment:* it supports what the day files already do (append, never rewrite), and it argues against letting the owl's consolidation become the record.
- **MIRIX-2.0: Multi-Agent Memory System.** 9 Nov. [arXiv 2611.12068](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12068). It leads on four of five memory benchmarks (91.4% on LoCoMo).
  - Complete records stay the system of record, and an index of episodes and facts cites their rows.
  - Retrieval runs under a fixed budget.
  - An offline step, "AutoDream", merges duplicates without rewriting any record.

  *Promising*, author benchmarks. **Bears on:** A, the owl. **Changes:** nothing. It reaches DISCERN's rule from another side: the night's consolidation sits beside the record and never replaces it.
- **GARP: Real-Time Generative Agents on Local Hardware in a Game Engine.** 8 Nov. [arXiv 2611.11064](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11064). Generative agents run in Godot on a local 3B model, with no cloud. Beside planning, memory and reflection, each agent has:
  - six needs that grow over time;
  - four Big Five personality axes;
  - an emotion appraised by the model;
  - a relationship with each other agent, updated after every conversation.

  It narrows 50 or more actions to 3–8, constrains answers with JSON schemas, and takes decisions ahead of need. 25 of 27 scenario runs passed log-based checks. *Promising.* **Bears on:** gap 1, H, section 3b. **Changes:** nothing. *Judgment:* the nearest local-hardware relative of our design; even deciding ahead of need matches the Pi publishing steps 10–15 minutes early. The per-pair relationship state it keeps is what the recognition test in section 3b would need.
- **MuSeToM: Multi-Sensory Evaluation of Belief Revision.** 12 Nov. [arXiv 2611.16662](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16662). 2,415 cases on whether someone's belief should change given what they perceived. Models often see that a belief may change but fail to name the new belief. They over-update on cues that are salient but insufficient. *Promising.* **Bears on:** D, T42, G3. **Changes:** nothing. It strengthens G3's premise: a plain-Qwen relevance check may say yes to vivid evidence that does not bear on the thesis.
- **Does Reasoning Improve Psychological Depth in Large Language Models?** 6 Nov. [arXiv 2611.09061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09061). In 60 blind pairs of short stories, seven human readers found no general advantage for reasoning models, and they agreed little among themselves (α 0.07). An LLM judge preferred the reasoning model in 98.3% of pairs, against 55.2% of human votes, partly tracking sentence length. *Promising*, small. **Bears on:** G, and the optional reasoning-token switches for writing, decisions and the owl (`README.md`). **Changes:** before any of those switches is adopted, judge it on blind human pairs matched for length. An LLM judge is not enough.
- **Prediction-Powered LLM Ranking with Adaptively Collected Human Feedback.** 9 Nov. [arXiv 2611.13186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13186). It combines many cheap LLM-judge ratings with a few human ones and keeps the inference valid, even when the human comparisons are chosen adaptively. *Promising*; the guarantees are asymptotic. **Bears on:** G, the ratchet. **Changes:** nothing now. *Judgment:* this is our situation, with few judgements by Welt and Claude and many cheap plain-Qwen ones. At 20–40 human items the intervals may not hold, so it suits a later test with a larger human sample.

## Dropped

11 papers: robot–IoT coordination, translation of heritage texts, coding-agent memory, recommender memory, homogenisation in teaching and in image generation, generative-model coverage, vision-language compression, security for coding agents.

Also dropped, with reasons:
- an exploratory study of memory consolidation (2611.15644): synthetic, and its own authors call it unreplicated;
- a CPU–GPU engine for mixture-of-experts models (Ostinato, 2611.11533): measured on four GPUs, and it matters only if a larger MoE model replaced dense Qwen.

All are in `seen.txt`.

## Changes to proposed experiments

- **T41 and G2:** score resolution apart from recall (right entity, current state, abstaining when the record is silent). Source: DISCERN.
- **Any reasoning-token switch:** blind human pairs matched for length before adoption; no LLM judge alone. Source: Psychological Depth.

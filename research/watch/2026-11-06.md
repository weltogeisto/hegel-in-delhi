# Literature watch, 6 November 2026

Papers first submitted 28 October to 6 November. 24 candidates from 33 queries; 7 kept.

## Kept

- **Measuring Model-Specific Alignment Drift in LLM-Based Opinion Dynamics.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.03980](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03980). Six models, with each agent's stance read by a fixed psychometric instrument, not by the model's own report.
  - Left alone, committed personas held their side for all 20 rounds in every model tested that way.
  - In discussion they drifted, so the loss is induced by interaction.
  - How far they drifted depended on the model: some kept opinion clusters apart, others abandoned them.

  *Promising.* **Bears on:** D, T8, T42, G4. **Changes:** *Judgment:* how fast his theses move is partly a property of the model, not only of the owl's rule. Any model change, including whatever T38 recommends, should rerun T8's thesis count on the candidate before it goes live.
- **Same Answer, Different Future: Matched Predictions Do Not Imply Matched Belief Updating.** 5 Nov. [arXiv 2611.07913](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07913). Two models can give the same prediction now and still respond differently to the next evidence. It depends on how the belief was acquired: from outcomes, from a summary, or from a stated rate. A summary carries a weight that barely grows with the number of observations it says it covers. *Promising*, but a toy task (Bernoulli inference, three open models). **Bears on:** D, A, T42, G3. **Changes:** *Judgment:* the owl hands each night's evidence on as a summary, so a summary of ten exchanges may count little more than one. G3 could put each evidence bundle in both forms, raw excerpts and the owl's summary, to see whether the form changes the verdict. That is 240 calls instead of 120, about 0.5 GPU hours.
- **MemRole: From Memories to Decisions for Long-Horizon Reasoning in Role-Playing Agents.** 1 Nov. [arXiv 2611.01778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01778). Role-playing agents imitate style well but fail to make decisions consistent with the character over a long story, even with past scenes in context. The training data limits each character's memory to what it could know at that point in the story. Their fix is trained (self-distillation). *Promising.* **Bears on:** A, B, G9, and gap 2. **Changes:** nothing. It confirms G9's question: memory in the prompt is not memory used in the decision. Training stays Welt's decision.
- **ACTOR: Action-Centered Temporal Organization for Literary Role-Playing Agents.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.03184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03184). Behaviour trajectories built from 143 novels, each real action set beside plausible actions out of character. Models struggle to infer actions consistent with the character from his earlier behaviour; preference training helps. *Promising.* **Bears on:** B, T43. **Changes:** nothing. *Judgment:* acting in character is harder than speaking in character, which fits commitments scoring 2.0–2.8 while voice sits closer. Its four-way next-action task needs a true next action; for us that would come only from his lives, which are kept for training.
- **Recursive Self-Improvement for Long-Horizon Role-Playing Dialogue.** 5 Nov. [arXiv 2611.07469](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07469).
  - A bounded updater revises a character's memory, its persona instructions and the policy that builds each prompt. It works as a versioned policy with acceptance gates and stopping conditions.
  - Over 100-round conversations, bad replies fell from 14.0% to 4.9%.
  - Blind human comparisons gave it 71% of decisive judgments.

  *Promising*, internal evaluation only. **Bears on:** B, G, the ratchet. **Changes:** nothing. *Judgment:* the closest published analogue to our weekly ratchet, with a model where Claude stands. Its acceptance gates are a check on our own: one change at a time, frozen inputs, a stop.
- **Creating Hybrid Interactive Narrative Systems with LLM.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.02807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02807). Story managers share a symbolic world of facts and operations, each operation with preconditions and effects. That lets any session be checked as a plan. No manager wins on agency, control, author intent and robustness at once. *Promising.* **Bears on:** gap 1, G8. **Changes:** nothing. It confirms G8's shape: what a voice claims is checked as an operation against the world's state.
- **EvoMemState: Evolving Memory State for Personal Agents.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.03743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03743). Facts are kept together with their validity and scope and with how they constrain or contradict earlier facts; an RL-trained step decides which apply now. *Promising.* **Bears on:** A, T41. **Changes:** nothing. Like last week's "Accept Changes, Reject Attacks", it confirms that T41 should keep which fact superseded which, and should not store facts alone.

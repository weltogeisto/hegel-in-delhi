# Literature watch, 15 November 2026

Papers first submitted 31 October to 15 November. 41 candidates from 33 queries; 11 kept. A run outside the Friday rhythm, made for the plan of the world's second phase (`WORLD-2.md`).

**Revised on 16 November.** The first version of this digest was written from abstracts. Nine of the eleven kept papers, and seven of the nine found beside them, have since been read in full (`research/WORLD-2-READINGS.md`). Several entries said more than their papers do. They are corrected below, and what was withdrawn is named. All are unreplicated preprints.

## Kept, and read in full

**Memory.**

- **DISCERN: Preserve at Write, Resolve at Read.** 11 Nov. [arXiv 2611.15729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15729). A store that only ever adds: it never merges two entities and never drops a superseded value. On its own synthetic benchmark a plain retrieval reader already matches the memory systems on keeping facts and on which value is current (0.93 to 0.98). DISCERN's lead is in telling entities apart (15 questions) and in declining to answer (F1 0.92 against 0.80). Its reader takes four turns, six tool calls and 7.6 seconds a question on a frontier model, and falls to 0.61–0.71 on open models of 120B and more. On a second benchmark plain retrieval fails at questions of time (0.08 against 0.71). *Promising.* **Bears on:** A, T41, G2. **Changes:** it confirms the day files as the archive. For T41: "not in the record" counts as right where the record holds nothing, and telling two people or orders apart is tested. **Withdrawn:** that "which value is current" is the unsolved part. Their own table shows it solved.
- **When Agents Need to Consolidate Memories.** 11 Nov. [arXiv 2611.15644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15644). Under a tight output budget a simple rule that prefers current, authoritative decisions recalled as many as the full system, given supplied keys and one shared ordering. Reserving a fifth of the budget for conflicts cost recall (0.44 to 0.34) and brought the conflicts only half back; the cost was nil only at 4,096 tokens. One team, synthetic fixtures, by its own account an engineering analysis. *Promising.* **Bears on:** A, T41. **Changes:** a fixed slice of his 850 characters for open obligations is our own arm, about 200 tokens where their smallest budget was 512. **Withdrawn:** "at a small cost in recall".

**Character.**

- **MemRole.** 1 Nov. [arXiv 2611.01778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01778). A fixed order of reasoning from memories to a decision, trained into 8B and 32B models, gains 15 points on the authors' own test, judged by a frontier model. Its self-distillation step needs a reference answer for every situation and a rubric a frontier model wrote. *Promising.* **Bears on:** B, training. **Changes:** nothing. It cannot run under our data rule: there is no reference for a moment in Delhi. **Withdrawn:** that it shows models imitate a style well and decide badly, which it does not measure; and that its memory pool is our 1831 gate.
- **ACTOR.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.03184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03184). A character's acts cut from 143 novels in story order, each with three plausible alternatives a model wrote. Untrained models pick the real act 57 to 78 times in 100, against a chance of 25; training on the pairs adds about 9 points. Taking away the character's profile or memory costs only 0.4 to 3.6 points, so most of the score comes from the scene and the wording of the options. *Promising.* **Bears on:** the Paris backtest. **Changes:** the next-act choice among four is usable for T50 only with the controls the paper lacks: the day removed, the order shuffled, and a human check of the invented alternatives. **Withdrawn:** "models choose badly among four".
- **Measuring Model-Specific Alignment Drift.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.03980](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03980). A persona committed to a side keeps its side for 20 rounds when it hears no one, in all five models tested, though it still moves a little. In discussion the share that keeps its side falls to between 0.60 and 1.00, by model. Six API models, one-sentence personas. The control removes the writing step as well as the peers. *Promising.* **Bears on:** D, T8, G4. **Changes:** G4 asks each question three ways: alone, after a conversation, and after writing alone, which is the owl's case and the one this paper does not test. Rerun on any change of model. **Withdrawn:** our inference that the owl's rule is the suspect. The evidence for that is our own reruns (T8), not this paper.

**Worlds.**

- **GARP.** 8 Nov. [arXiv 2611.11064](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11064). The generative-agents loop on a 3B model on a laptop: six needs, 50 actions narrowed to three to eight before the model is asked, and relationships the model moves by naming a change that code maps to a fixed step. 25 of 27 runs pass checks on the logs (nine scenarios, three runs each). The noise of the model's reading swallowed an effect the authors had built in, and retelling changed the hour or the owner of a commitment. *Promising*, a platform paper with no raters. **Changes:** nothing. It is a reason to start his people's regard from recorded acts, and to pass on the engine's fact and never a retelling.
- **Creating Hybrid Interactive Narrative Systems with LLM.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.02807](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02807). A shared world of operations with preconditions and effects, and three managers over it. A manager that repaired dead ends applied 27% of a simulated player's commands unchanged, against 44%; characters acting after the player left 68% and almost nothing of the author's planned course. One scenario, one model as player. *Promising.* **Bears on:** I, world design. **Changes:** it gives the storylines of `WORLD-2.md` their form. **Withdrawn:** that it names what our no-steering rule protects. Its "agency" is the share of typed commands accepted.

**Judging with few judges.**

- **AGORA.** 3 Nov. [arXiv 2611.05556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05556). An item-response model over a pool of judges. The smallest pool tested is five judges and the smallest set 50 items, on absolute ratings; against a plain mean the gain at 50 items is under 0.01. *Promising.* **Bears on:** G. **Changes:** nothing. It does not apply to one person and one or two models.
- **Prediction-Powered LLM Ranking.** 9 Nov. [arXiv 2611.13186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13186). Valid intervals from human and machine judgments together, in theory and in simulation. No experiment uses fewer than 4,000 human labels, and the human labels are themselves simulated by a model. *Promising* as a method. **Changes:** nothing. **Withdrawn, for both:** that they are a way to close more experiments a week with Welt's few pairs. The procedure the plan now uses is in its chapter 1.

## Known from the abstract only

- **EvoMemState.** 2 Nov. [arXiv 2611.03743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03743). Facts carry a state beside them: whether they still hold, in what scope. **Changes:** nothing.
- **Recursive Self-Improvement for Long-Horizon Role-Playing Dialogue.** 5 Nov. [arXiv 2611.07469](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07469). An updater of a character's memory and instructions, written as a versioned policy with acceptance gates. Internal evaluation only. **Changes:** nothing.
- **Route to Remember.** 3 Nov. [arXiv 2611.05636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05636). Facts moved into the weights by self-distillation. For us that would be training every night on his own history: a hard limit. Not proposed.

## Missed by the search, found the same day

Read in full unless marked.

- **Replay Fidelity in LLM Agent Evaluation.** Liu and Lu, 8 Nov. [arXiv 2611.11216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11216). Recorded code of 71 failed agent runs, executed again. 42 matched their archive with the declared seed, and 68 once the random generator was reset before every step. Four runs matched in output and verdict and still left different rows in the database. No model is called again, so it says nothing about forking a live agent. **Changes:** a saved morning holds the engine's state and the seeds, and forks are compared by state as well as by text.
- **Compile as Hypothesis.** Yang and Li, 31 Oct. [arXiv 2611.01135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01135). Structured prompt search gave no significant gain on strong reasoning models. A free-text optimiser gained 0.14 on one model and one task, and about a quarter of that was the scoring rubric rebuilt in the prompt: one ablation, on 20 held-out examples. A gateway silently served another model during outages. **Changes:** the judge's rubric stays out of any search, and every call logs the model that served it.
- **Masked Erosion.** So et al., 28 Oct. [arXiv 2610.38392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38392). A gate that looks only at success accepts the deletion of a redundant safety rule 63 times in 100. Removing such a rule alone shows no loss; that is what masking is. What held was freezing the rules and testing declared harms with each rule removed. **Changes:** protective rules are named, frozen and probed. **Withdrawn:** switching each old rule off once, alone, which cannot show a masked rule.
- **Evidence-Governed Adoption.** Lonkar, 12 Nov. [arXiv 2611.16378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16378). Fresh paired audits, declared protected abilities, a small first deployment. Its guarantee for a first candidate needs some 17,000 audit observations and 68,000 for calibration. No experiment on a real agent. **Changes:** nothing. The ratchet borrows the practice and cannot claim the proof.
- **We Are What We Remember (MindMem).** 12 Nov. [arXiv 2611.16223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16223). A system handed the right memory answered 97% of questions about a shared past and used it in half of the moments that called for it. A synthetic benchmark. **Changes:** we score the use of a shared past, by a measure of our own.
- **Knowledge in the Harness, Reliability in the Weights.** 11 Nov. [arXiv 2611.15268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15268). Without a teacher model, rules made unsolved tasks solvable and training made solved ones reliable. **Changes:** nothing. It is the plan's order: structure first.
- **Wrong the Same Way.** 9 Nov. [arXiv 2611.13218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13218). Training on a checker's verdict entrenches a wrong answer the checker accepts across many prompts. Shown in a model of 100,000 parameters. **Changes:** before any adapter is trained on the audit, its misses are counted by hand, and the ones that repeat across scenes are looked for after.
- **From the abstract only:** *LLMs Don't Know What They Know* ([arXiv 2611.06808](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06808)) and a paper on repeated names in generated fiction ([arXiv 2611.01284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01284)).

## Changes to proposed experiments

- **T41:** "not in the record" counts as right where the record holds nothing; two people or two orders must be told apart; one arm of our own with a fixed slice for open obligations.
- **G2:** the raw-search arm stays a hypothesis. One study found reranked raw turns within three points of extracted memory at our budget, with a closed reranker and with embeddings. It gets a rerank arm and a date arm that can be compared, and a count of answers given where none was due.
- **G4:** each question alone, after a conversation and after writing alone; a challenge to each thesis ("Are you sure?"); rerun on any change of model.
- **G8, now T57 in `WORLD-2.md`:** the check on what a voice claims is the engine's audit, with a reader that is not the writer. Its misses are counted by hand.
- **T42:** an encounter has one entry, and the gist and his recall point to it. Counting each source once is a rule for the engine; models do not do it unaided.
- **T50:** a next-act choice among four, with the day removed as a control.
- **New, from `WORLD-2.md`:** the world's own experiments are forks from one saved morning, with a control fork.

All wait for Welt's word, as before.
